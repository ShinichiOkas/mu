## 2. Synthesis for 'mu'
The project "mu" is defined by a **minimalism principle**, requiring a foundation that balances low resource usage with extremely low operational friction.

- **Feature Analysis**: 
    - **Ollama** provides an "experience layer" that eliminates the manual toil of model quantization and configuration while maintaining the performance of the underlying engines (llama.cpp/MLX).
    - **llama.cpp** offers the absolute minimum overhead but increases the "setup tax" (manual model management).
    - **vLLM** is designed for high-throughput production and is over-engineered for a minimal personal agent.
    - **LM Studio** is GUI-centric, which contradicts the headless/minimalist nature of an agent framework.

- **Gap Analysis**: While llama.cpp is technically "more minimal" in terms of binary size and dependency, Ollama is "more minimal" in terms of **developer cognitive load** and **integration effort**.
