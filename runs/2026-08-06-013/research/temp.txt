## 3. Synthesis for 'mu' (Minimalist Agent)

Project 'mu' prioritizes minimalism in its operation and deployment. 

- **Resource Usage**: While `llama.cpp` is the most lean, `Ollama` provides a negligible overhead while maintaining the ability to run quantized models on consumer hardware.
- **Integration**: `Ollama` provides the most seamless OpenAI-compatible API experience, which is critical for agentic frameworks.
- **Operational Overhead**: `Ollama`'s "Docker-style" model management (`pull`/`run`) drastically reduces the complexity of updating or switching models compared to manual GGUF handling in `llama.cpp`.
- **Performance**: For a single-user agent like 'mu', the massive throughput of `vLLM` is unnecessary overhead.

## 4. 邨占ｫ・(Conclusion)

Based on the requirement for a "minimalist" foundation:
**Ollama should be continued as the foundation for project 'mu'.**

It offers the best balance of "Setup ease" and "API compatibility" without sacrificing the "Low resource" capabilities provided by its underlying `llama.cpp` engine. The operational simplicity of Ollama aligns perfectly with the minimalist philosophy of 'mu'.
