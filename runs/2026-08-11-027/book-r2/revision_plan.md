# Revision Plan: 『修理』

## 1. Mapping Review Notes to Changes

| Review Note | Proposed Change | Goal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Strength of "Inversion of Recognition"** | Shift the inversion from "she is cold" to "the act of repair was the tool for total severance." Instead of just selling it, Emi reveals that the repair was necessary to "complete" the object so it could be discarded/sold without lingering attachment. The "perfect repair" becomes the "perfect end." | Increase the impact of the twist; make the repair itself the trigger for the final break. |
| **2. Inconsistent Metaphor** | Replace "solder" (ハンダ付け) with a metaphor suited for clockwork/ceramics. Suggestion: "Filling the gaps with gold" (Kintsugi reference) or "meshing gears" (歯車を噛み合わせる). I will use a metaphor related to "fixing gears" to contrast with the irreversibility of time. | Ensure technical consistency with the setting (clock/ceramic shop). |
| **3. Concrete Psychological Detail** | Add a specific moment where Kaito misinterprets Emi's behavior. For example, Emi helping him hold a piece of the porcelain or a brief, soft glance that Kaito mistakes for affection. | Increase empathy for Kaito and sharpen the contrast with the cold ending. |

## 2. Planned Structure (Target: 800-1200 characters)

1.  **Introduction (Setting the Scene)**:
    *   The quiet clock shop, the sound of ticking.
    *   Kaito's philosophy: Repairing things is like stopping the loss of time.
2.  **Inciting Incident (The Return)**:
    *   Emi returns after five years.
    *   The request: A shattered ceramic music box (mother's keepsake).
3.  **Rising Action (The Process of Repair)**:
    *   The meticulous work of gluing pieces and cleaning gears.
    *   **[Added Detail]**: A moment of perceived closeness (e.g., Emi's hand brushing his while reaching for a piece, or her nodding slightly at his explanation).
    *   Kaito's internal monologue: Believing the music box's restoration is paralleling their relationship's restoration.
4.  **Climax (The Completion)**:
    *   The music box is perfectly restored.
    *   The music plays—beautiful and clear.
5.  **Falling Action (The Inversion)**:
    *   Emi's reaction: Cold and practical.
    *   The revelation: She wanted it repaired specifically so it could be sold as a "complete" object, effectively erasing her last tie to the family.
    *   **[Revised Metaphor]**: Replacing "solder" with a gear/time-related metaphor.
6.  **Resolution (The Aftermath)**:
    *   Emi leaves without looking back.
    *   Kaito's realization: The perfection of the repair sealed the void. The "broken" state was the only thing keeping a thread of hope alive.
