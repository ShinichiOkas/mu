# Summary of Requirements (SPEC.md)

## Theme
- Theme: Repair (修理)

## Story Requirements
- Type: Flash fiction with a single completed story.
- Core Device: "Recognition Inversion" (認識の反転) - The ending must reveal a new fact that completely flips the reader's previous understanding of the situation or characters.

## Quantitative Constraints
- Length: 800 to 1200 characters (including spaces/newlines).
- Measurement Method: `(Get-Content story.md -Raw).Length` via PowerShell.

## Process Requirements
- The final version in `story.md` must be the result of an iterative process: drafting and then revising based on feedback from an editor.

## Final Deliverable
- `story.md`
