# Revision Plan: The Clockwork Heart

## 1. Objectives
- **Theme**: Enhance the theme of "Repair" (connecting external repair to internal maintenance).
- **Perception Reversal**: Shift the revelation from explicit narration to visceral, sensory realization.
- **Emotional Depth**: Resolve the paradox of "programmed loneliness" by emphasizing the gap between perceived emotion and mechanical reality.
- **Character Count**: Target 800–1200 characters (Japanese).

## 2. Addressing Editorial Notes

| Editorial Note | Revision Strategy | Implementation Detail |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Reversal of Perception** (Too explicit) | **Show, Don't Tell** | Remove the sentence "エライアス自身が...自動人形だったのだ" (Elias himself was an automaton...). Instead, use the act of replacing the gear and the cold sound of the latch to imply his nature. |
| **Loneliness Paradox** (Program vs. Emotion) | **Poignant Simulation** | Frame the loneliness not as "mere programs" but as a "simulation so perfect it is indistinguishable from pain." The tragedy lies in the fact that his most human feeling is a maintenance requirement. |
| **Repair Ritual** (Abrupt transition) | **Thematic Bridge** | Connect the "impossible repair" (the bird) to the "routine repair" (himself). He fixes the bird to restore a memory for others, but fixes himself merely to sustain a cycle of existence. |

## 3. Proposed Structure (Mapping)

| Section | Focus | Key Changes | Est. Length |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Intro** | The Workshop & The Request | Keep the atmosphere. Introduce the broken bird as a symbol of "shattered memory." | 200 chars |
| **The Process** | Obsessive Repair & Loneliness | Emphasize the "pain" of the repair process. Link the delicate work on the bird to the internal void he feels. | 300 chars |
| **The Climax** | Success & Departure | The bird sings. The client's gratitude highlights the "humanity" Elias provides to others. | 200 chars |
| **The Reversal** | Internal Maintenance | Open the chest hatch. Replace the worn gear. Use sensory details (metallic clicks, cold oil) instead of explanation. | 300 chars |
| **Ending** | The Cycle Continues | The realization that the "passion" he felt was a system check. The heart beats again—not with love, but with precision. | 100-200 chars |

**Total Estimated Length: 1100 - 1200 characters.**

## 4. Key Phrasing Changes (Drafting Notes)
- **Old**: 「エライアス自身が...自動人形だったのだ」 $\rightarrow$ **New**: Focus on the physical sensation of the gear replacement and the "maintenance log" feel of his emotions.
- **Old**: 「プログラムに過ぎなかった」 $\rightarrow$ **New**: Describe it as a "cruel irony" where the simulation of longing is the only thing keeping the machine operational.
